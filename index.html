Services

GSA container, vault, and lock service, done to standard.

From a combination change on one container to a vault door replacement, our certified technicians do the work themselves.

Container & Vault Maintenance

General and preventive maintenance on GSA-approved security containers and vault doors, keeping them operating and compliant.

Combination Changes

Mechanical and electronic combination changes, including personnel turnover and scheduled changes.

Lock Repair & Replacement

Repair and replacement of high-security locks, including FF-L-2740B electromechanical locks and time locks.

Emergency Openings

Openings for lockouts, forgotten combinations, and failed locks, followed by repair so the container goes back into service.

Vault Door Installation

Vault door installation and replacement, plus electronic vault and safe lock installation and service.

SCIF Doors

Installation, maintenance, and repair of STC- and RF-rated SCIF doors, including alignment and hardware.

About

A veteran-owned specialty shop.

GSA Vault and Container Repair focuses on one thing: the security containers, vaults, locks, and SCIF doors that protect classified material on federal installations.

The work grew out of a family locksmith business that has serviced timelocks, safe locks, padlocks, and locking devices since 2002 and later specialized in GSA security equipment. Today we bring that experience to the West Coast and the Pacific.

We are part of the Bischer Group family of companies.
